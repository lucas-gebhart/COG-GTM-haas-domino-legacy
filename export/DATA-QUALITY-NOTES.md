## Intentional defects

### 1. Free-text status / stage values (wart #1)

* `Request.Status`: 35 documents carry a non-keyword value (10 distinct variants). Variants and counts:
  * `COMPLETE`: 9
  * `CXL`: 1
  * `Complete `: 4
  * `SHIPPED`: 2
  * `cancel`: 1
  * `canceled`: 1
  * `closed`: 6
  * `completed`: 5
  * `ship`: 1
  * `shiped`: 5
* `AwardsCase.Stage`: 162 documents carry a non-keyword value:
  * `CLOSED`: 43
  * `Closed `: 44
  * `Complete`: 33
  * `closed`: 42
* `AwardLine.AwardName` free-text (unknown import code kept as name): 40

### 2. Duplicated requester documents (wart #2)

* vetmedals `Requester`: 200 of 2,500 documents (8.0%) were generated as a variant of an existing requester (suffix, middle initial in first name, ZIP+4 vs ZIP5, initial-only first name, upper-cased surname).
* Of those, 81 share an identical `LookupKey` with another document (the dedupe key failed to prevent them), and 44 were later marked `MergedInto` by a CSR; the remainder are live duplicates.

### 3. Mixed date formats (wart #3)

* `AwardsCase.AuthorizationDate` stored as text: 64 (shapes `M/D/YYYY`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD MON YY`, `YYYYMMDD`)
* `ShipmentRecord.ShippedDate` stored as text: 223; `ShipDateText` populated on 427 records
* `Request.EnteredDate` stored as text: 7

### 4. Deleted-but-referenced vendor (wart #4)

* Vendor `1K7Q3` (Colonial Flag & Banner Works) has no `Vendor` document. Referenced by `Request.VendorKey` on 25 requests (all with empty `VendorName`) and by 64 `RequestLine` documents.
* Vendor `1PRQ2` exists but is `Active = No`; still referenced by 0 requests.

### 5. Orphaned child documents (wart #5)

* `RequestLine` whose parent UNID is not in the export: 40
* `AwardLine` whose parent UNID is not in the export: 60
* `EngravingJob` whose `CaseNumber` matches no `AwardsCase`: 9
* `ShipmentRecord` whose `CaseNumber` matches no `AwardsCase`: 8

### 6. `$FILE` attachment references (wart #6)

* heraldry.nsf `$FILE` items: 319 (scanned DD1348-6 PDFs, justification memos, SF-50s). Binary content is not in the export.
* vetmedals.nsf `$FILE` items: 130 (raw authorization transmissions, correspondence).
* `AuthorizationFile` documents whose `FileBody` should hold an attachment but have no `$FILE` item: 4
* Only the 4 files in `export/authorization-files/` exist as real bytes.

### 9. Non-unique business keys (wart #9)

* Duplicate `Request.DocumentNumber`: 31 extra documents across 27 numbers
* Duplicate `AwardsCase.CaseNumber`: 9 extra documents

## Synthetic-data statement

All personal names are drawn from a constructed surname list; addresses combine real city/state/ZIP3 prefixes with generated
street numbers and names; service numbers and SSN fragments are random. Unit designations, installations, medal names and
campaign streamer names are real public information. No record describes a real person.
